Story Process A Note

Origin

It began with a watch that needed sizing.

Henrik & Co did not start as a name. It started with a strap, a wrist, and a crystal that was cracked. The Velaro Chronograph is what that attention became.

The story

Four small beginnings.

01

How it began

It started simply. He sized his mother's watch, then his brother's.

The work was ordinary: a length of strap, a wrist that asked for a little more room or a little less. A watch to be worn, and nothing further in mind. It was something that needed doing, and he did it.

02

The broken watch

He was given a watch that could no longer be worn as it was. The crystal was cracked.

He bought a replacement band. Then, knowing nothing about watches, he decided he would fix the crystal himself.

03

Learning the craft

That first repair did not stay a single afternoon. Once the crystal was whole, he wanted to understand the rest.

He set out to learn watchmaking from the ground up: how a watch comes apart, how the parts go back together, and how much patience a small machine asks for.

04

A watch of his own

Studying the craft led, in time, to a design of his own. That watch became Henrik & Co.

The first of them is the Velaro Chronograph. What follows is how it was drawn, proved, and given its color.

The design process

From a plain case to the Velaro.

The first build, in the order it took shape. A case with nothing on it. A dial before any type. A drawing for where the words would sit. A proof from the maker. Then the color of the hands.

Colophon

A note on the making

The founder brought the idea, and the photographs of this first build, to North, his AI chief of staff. North designed the page and produced the render of the Velaro. The story was already his. This is only how it was set down.
